# Literature positioning

This pilot tests whether an offline finite-budget composite evaluator of
pi_z^m ⊕ pi_g has learnable signal. It is not a claim of novelty.

Baselines, not innovations:
- ordinary FQE
- finite-horizon FQE
- distributional OPE
- ensemble uncertainty / LCB
- fallback-to-baseline / SPIBB-style rejection
- IQL expectile value (B0)

References for positioning only:
- Off-Policy Fitted Q-Evaluation... ICML 2022
- Distributional Offline Policy Evaluation... ICML 2023
- Safe Policy Improvement with Baseline Bootstrapping, ICML 2019
- Offline RL with Implicit Q-Learning, ICLR 2022

A paper-level contribution would still need to show independent necessity of
composite switching, finite remaining budget, and relative pairwise estimation.
This round does not authorize that claim.
